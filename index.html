The two scores

What moved it

Every input starts at a neutral 50. Bars to the right push up, bars to the left pull down. The two scores steer different things, so they're kept apart.

Bull score

Today's conditions. Moves the next 12 to 18 months, then fades. It barely changes the $1M date.

Adoption trend

Who keeps accumulating, year over year. Speeds up or slows the long-run trend line for decades, so it moves the $1M date most.

    The manual

    How the $1M date is made

    Five steps, filled in with today's numbers. Every figure on this page comes out of these steps.

    Bull scoreA 0–100 grade of today's conditions. 50 means nothing is pushing either way. It shapes the next year, then fades.
    Adoption trendA 0–100 grade of who is accumulating year over year: ETFs, companies, miners. It changes how fast the trend line rises for decades.
    Trend line (fair value)Where Bitcoin "should" trade ignoring the cycle. Fitted to the midpoint of every cycle since 2013.
    Meter priceThe trend line plus where we are in the 4-year cycle plus today's bull score. What the model thinks BTC should trade at right now.
    Most likely monthThe median. Half of the 2,000 futures reach $1M by then, half later.
    Surprise shocksRare, lasting jumps in either direction: a nation starts buying (+20%), or a major crackdown or failure (−25%).
    Fat tailsBitcoin has more extreme months than a bell curve predicts. The random moves are drawn to allow that.
    Is this a prediction?No. It shows what the inputs and assumptions imply, and the honesty panel shows how much those assumptions matter.
